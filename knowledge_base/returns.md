# Returns & Refunds

## Return window
You can return most unused items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in their original packaging with all accessories.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened coffee beans, ground coffee, tea, syrups and other consumables cannot be returned for hygiene and freshness reasons unless they arrived damaged or are the wrong item. Gift cards and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned. Grinder burrs and gaskets that have been installed cannot be returned.

## Used equipment
Espresso machines and grinders that have been used can be returned within 30 days only if they are faulty. Faulty equipment is covered by the warranty policy instead of the return policy after 30 days.

## How to start a return
Go to Account → Orders, choose the order and click "Start a return". You will receive a prepaid return label for US orders. International customers pay for return shipping unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection. Your bank may take a further 3–10 business days to show the credit. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want. If the item is faulty and still under warranty, we can ship a replacement instead.

## Restocking fee
Opened espresso machines returned in working condition within 30 days have a 10% restocking fee. Unopened items have no restocking fee.
