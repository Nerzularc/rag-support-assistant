# Shipping & Delivery

## Shipping destinations
Brewline Coffee Supply ships to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and all EU countries. We do not currently ship to Russia, Brazil or India. PO boxes are accepted in the US and Australia only.

## Processing time
Orders placed before 2 PM US Eastern Time on a business day are packed the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, on weekends or on US public holidays are packed the next business day. Pre-order items ship on the date shown on the product page.

## Shipping rates and free shipping
Standard shipping inside the United States is free on orders of $60 or more after discounts. Below $60, standard US shipping is a flat $6.95. Express US shipping (1–2 business days) costs $18. International rates are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination; orders over $150 to Canada, the UK and Australia ship free with standard international service.

## Delivery times
Standard US delivery takes 3–5 business days. Express US delivery takes 1–2 business days. International standard delivery takes 7–14 business days. Delivery times are estimates from the carrier and are not guaranteed.

## Tracking your order
A tracking link is emailed as soon as the label is created. You can also find it under Account → Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Customs, duties and taxes
International orders may be charged import duties or taxes by the destination country. These are paid by the recipient to the carrier and are not included in our prices, except for UK and EU orders, where VAT is collected at checkout and no extra fee is charged on delivery.

## Lost or damaged packages
If tracking shows delivered but you cannot find the package, wait 48 hours and check with neighbours, then contact us. If an item arrives damaged, email a photo of the item and the box to support@brewline.example within 7 days of delivery and we will send a replacement or refund at no cost.
