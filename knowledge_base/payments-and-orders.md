# Payments & Orders

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay. For orders over $250 in the US you can pay in four interest-free instalments with Shop Pay Installments, subject to approval.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can change the shipping address or cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it from the order confirmation page. After 1 hour the order may already be packed; contact support and we will try, but cannot guarantee changes.

## Discount codes
Only one discount code can be used per order. Discount codes cannot be applied to gift cards, subscriptions or items already on sale. If you forgot to apply a code, contact us within 7 days of ordering and we will refund the difference.

## Price matching
We match the price of identical new items sold by authorised US retailers within 14 days of your purchase. Send us a link to the lower price. Marketplace sellers, auctions and clearance prices are excluded.

## Invoices and business orders
A PDF invoice is available from Account → Orders. Businesses that order more than $2,000 per year can apply for a wholesale account with net-30 payment terms by emailing wholesale@brewline.example.
