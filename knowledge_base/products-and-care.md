# Products & Machine Care

## Choosing an espresso machine
The Brewline Solo is a single-boiler machine for beginners who mainly drink espresso. The Brewline Duo has a dual boiler so you can steam milk and pull shots at the same time, which suits people who make lattes and cappuccinos daily. Both machines need a 15 A household outlet and a grinder capable of fine espresso grind.

## Choosing a grinder
For espresso you need a burr grinder with fine stepless or micro-step adjustment, such as the Brewline Precision 64. Blade grinders are not suitable for espresso. For filter coffee the Brewline Everyday 40 is enough.

## Descaling
Use Brewline Descaler or any citric-acid-based descaler. Do not use vinegar, which can damage gaskets and leave a taste. Descaling takes about 30 minutes; the full steps are in the manual under "Maintenance".

## Backflushing
Backflush machines with a three-way solenoid valve (the Brewline Duo) once a week with plain water and once a month with espresso machine cleaner. The Brewline Solo does not have a solenoid valve and must not be backflushed with detergent.

## Water
Use filtered water with hardness between 50 and 120 ppm. Distilled water is not recommended because some machines need minerals to detect the water level.

## Coffee freshness
Beans taste best between 7 and 30 days after the roast date. Store them in an airtight container away from light and heat. Do not store beans in the fridge.
