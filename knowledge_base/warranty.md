# Warranty

## Warranty length
Espresso machines sold by Brewline carry a 2-year limited warranty from the delivery date. Electric grinders carry a 1-year warranty. Manual grinders and brewing accessories such as kettles and scales carry a 1-year warranty. Refurbished machines carry a 6-month warranty.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use, including pump failure, heating element failure, electronic control faults and leaks from internal components.

## What the warranty does not cover
The warranty does not cover damage from limescale build-up caused by not descaling, use of unfiltered hard water, commercial use, drops or impacts, unauthorised repairs or modifications, or normal wear parts such as gaskets, shower screens and burrs.

## How to make a warranty claim
Email support@brewline.example with your order number, a short description of the problem and a video showing the fault. Our technicians respond within 2 business days. If the claim is approved we either repair the unit, ship a replacement part you can install yourself, or replace the machine. We pay shipping both ways for approved claims in the US.

## Descaling requirement
To keep the warranty valid, descale your espresso machine at least every 3 months, or every month if your water hardness is above 120 ppm. Keep your descaler receipt or order history as proof.
