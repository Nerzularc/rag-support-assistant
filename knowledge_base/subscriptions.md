# Coffee Subscriptions

## How the subscription works
The Brewline Roaster's Choice subscription delivers freshly roasted beans every 2, 3 or 4 weeks. Each bag is roasted within 48 hours before it ships. You can choose whole bean or a grind size for espresso, filter, French press or cold brew.

## Pricing
A 340 g bag costs $16 per delivery and a 1 kg bag costs $42 per delivery. Subscription shipping is always free in the United States. Subscribers get 10% off all equipment orders while the subscription is active.

## Skipping, pausing and changing deliveries
You can skip a delivery, change the frequency or change the grind any time up to 2 days before the next roast date from Account → Subscriptions. You can pause for up to 3 months.

## Cancelling a subscription
You can cancel any time from Account → Subscriptions → Cancel. There is no cancellation fee and no minimum number of deliveries. Cancelling within 2 days of the roast date will still ship the current order.

## Billing
You are charged on the roast date for each delivery. If a payment fails we retry after 2 days and email you; after three failed attempts the subscription is paused.
