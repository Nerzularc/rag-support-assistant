# Contact & Support Hours

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM US Eastern Time. Email replies are sent within 1 business day. Live chat with a human agent is available during support hours.

## How to reach us
Email support@brewline.example for orders, returns and warranty. Email wholesale@brewline.example for business accounts. Phone support is available at +1 (555) 010-2040 during support hours for orders over $500.

## Company details
Brewline Coffee Supply is a fictional demo company used for this project. Our warehouse is located in Portland, Oregon, USA. There is no showroom and we do not offer in-person pickup.
